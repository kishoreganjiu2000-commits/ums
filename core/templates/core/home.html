About the university

Built for teaching first

Founded in 1998, {{ SITE_NAME }} has grown from a single faculty of science into a full university with six faculties and more than four thousand students. The founding decision has not changed: lecture groups stay small, teaching staff hold terminal degrees, and every programme carries a substantial project component.

Alongside teaching, twelve research centres work with regional industry and public institutions on transport, health, agriculture and education technology.

  • Accredited programmes with external review every five years
  • Full-time faculty only — no teaching delivered by contractors
  • Scholarships covering up to 100% of tuition for merit students
  • Library access, career services and counselling included for all students
